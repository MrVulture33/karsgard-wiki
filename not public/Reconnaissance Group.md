The **Reconnaissance Group** is a subdivision of the [[Special Tactics Branch]], they are overseen by their Commander who answers to the [[Missions Administrator]]. The purpose of this subdivision is to gather wartime intelligence on enemy movement, paramilitary activity or valuable locations and pass it on to relevant individuals. When passing information on becomes a monumental task, the group is expected to carry out operations by themselves.
