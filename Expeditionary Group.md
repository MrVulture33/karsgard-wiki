The **Expeditionary Group** is a subdivision of the [[Special Tactics Branch]], they are overseen by
their Commander who answers to the [[Missions Administrator]]. The purpose of this subdivision is to be released without a tether behind enemy lines. There they will locate and destroy vital positions such as bridges, roads and, towers. Eliminating enemy supplies such as armor, weapons, food and entertainment.
