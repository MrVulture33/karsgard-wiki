Walker and Wright Holdings is a [[Corporation]] classified as a [[Private Equity Firm]], it was
founded following the [[privatization]] of [[Blackguard]] by **Anne Rendahl Walker** and **Andreas**
**Wright**. In 1432, they retired as co-executives of the company and signed [[Sven Clausen]],
minority shareholder, as [[Chief Executive Officer|CEO]] of the corporation, while they retained their shares.

Walker and Wright Holdings own a controlling share in the following companies:

- [[Standard Pulp]]
- [[Blackpowder Ltd]]
- [[Hewell Hazys]]
- [[Weiss Lending]]

And they own a large share in the following other companies:

- [[Marigold Casino]]
- [[Atrexian Steel]]
- [[Wimbleton Shipping]]
- [[Dunne Coal]]
- [[Broadsheet Guild]]
- [[Underrail Transport & Cargo]]
