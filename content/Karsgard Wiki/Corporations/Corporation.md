The [[2nd Charter of Trade]] defines corporate entities, its fiduciary duties to provide a profit for
shareholders, including shareholder rights such as providing voting rights based on owned
stock percentages, and trading shares.

It would eventually give rise to [[Private Equity Firm]], as well as the goods giants like [[Atrexian Steel]], [[Blackpowder Ltd]], and [[Standard Pulp]].
