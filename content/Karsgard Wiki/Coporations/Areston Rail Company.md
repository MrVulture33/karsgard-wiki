The **Areston Rail Company** is a publicly and privately owned [[Atrexian]] [[Corporation]]. It's a co-venture between the [[Atrexian Republic]] and the [[Areston Confederate Union]].

The Corporation was founded by the [[Treaty of Flintwood]], and its bylaws created. The Treaty outlines a Board of Directors, and grandes one seat for each [[Nation-States|nation]] to be appointed by the [[President of Atrexia]] and the [[Chamberlain of the Union]] respectively. Two other board seats are granted to a pair of corporate investors, the [[Mallory Corporation]] and the [[Rowe Corporation]], who received a lump-sum payment for their rail roads.

This Board of Directors then elects a [[Chief Executive Officer|CEO]] for the corporation, who themselves gets a board seat. The Board has the power to dismiss the CEO with a majority vote at any meeting.

Every year, the CEO determines policy for the corporation, as well as deciding how much to pay in dividends, to be divided equally between the four shareholders.

Historically, the corporations have preferred CEOs that promise to pay loads of dividends, whereas the Republic has preferred ones that invest and the Confederate Union tends to be the deciding vote.

## Train Routes
Trains are made to run at an average speed of 24 miles an hour, with the corporation
preferring to keep them reliable rather than quick.

A train ticket is [[Currency|N200]].

There are six train routes that go back and forth from their respective end stations, each
train leaves at 7 o' clock in the morning, crosses with their partner train in the middle on
to make a swap, and then arrives at its end station.

From Blackguard to Cliffhold: 12 hours.
From Cliffhold to Highwarf: 8 hours.
From Blackguard to Fort Oldfield: 8 hours.
From Fort Oldfield to Northpass: 12 hours.
From Blackguard to Hatchmere: 10 hours.
