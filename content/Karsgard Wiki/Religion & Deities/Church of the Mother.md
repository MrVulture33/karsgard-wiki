A religion that started in the nation of [[Atrexia]] that reveres the **Archmother**, a goddess of order, control, and innovation. The church’s ranks are divided as follows:

- Matriarch, 1 per church
- Nursemothers, 5 per church
- Child, maximum 20 per church
- Nascent, no maximum
