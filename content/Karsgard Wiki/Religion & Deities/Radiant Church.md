A religion that started 700 years ago near La Manchaland, as a response to the creatures of the night that prowl there. It has extended its reach to other [[Seven Realms|realms]] of [[Mundane World of Karsgard|Karsgard]], mainly to [[Draskhut]] and [[Atrexia]], the latter being the less present in.

The church's ranks are divided as follows:

- High-Apostle
- Deacon
- Bishop
- Cardinal
- Priest

The **Radiant Church** is also divided into subgroups called **Radiant Orders** that tend to do different things within the interests of the church. These groups are the **Militia**, the **Blood Cross**, and the **Grey Templars**.

Ranks in the Militia

- Inquisitor General, commands a division of Captains
- Inquisitor Captain, commands a brigade of Sergeants
- Inquisitor Sargent, commands a battalion of Infantry
- Inquisitor Infantry

Ranks in the Grey Templars

- Grey (Leader)
- Red (Elite force)
- Yellow (Heavy arms)
- White (Initiate)
