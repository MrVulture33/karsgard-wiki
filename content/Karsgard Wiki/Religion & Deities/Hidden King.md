Pseudo cult all around [[Mundane World of Karsgard|Karsgard]] that reveres an entity they called the *Hidden King*, the *One Who Waits* or the *King in Yellow*. Their followers obey any order they receive from the King, even if it means taking their own lives, but only a small number of these followers can directly hear the King’s voice.

Those that are chosen by the King to hear His voice are used as tools by Him.

The King also talks about a place called [[Carcosa]], which is [[Carcosa|Heaven]].
