This religion started in the early feudal kingdoms of [[Atrexia]], and survived until modern day. It reveres the Twelve Sons and of course Patrem, their father (although some believe that Patrem is another name for the [[Church of the Mother|Archmother]]).

Each Son has a domain of its own, while Patrem embodies parenthood, discipline, and courage.

- **Primuro**, the youngest. Male presented (Travel, Beauty domain)
- **Diastolia**, the eldest. Female presented (Change, Death domain)
- **Tridentarius**, the fierce. Female presented (Grave, War domain)
- **Tetrares**, the nurturer. Male presented (Peace, Life domain)
- **Quinneset**, the just. Ambiguous presented (Reason, Justice domain)
- **Sexteron**, the self-indulgent. Male presented (Madness, Rum domain)
- **Heptimus**, the crafter. Male presented (Fire, Forge domain)
- **Octakiseron**, the devastating. Female presented (Tempest, Destruction domain)
- **Nonagesimus**, the opulent. Female presented (Reason, Wealth domain)
- **Decaron**, the corrupt. Ambiguous presented (Pestilence, Blood domain)
- **Undecai**, the nocturnal. Female presented (Night, Hunt domain)
- **Dodecei**, the diurnal. Male presented (Light, Trickery domain)

Patrem domains: Love, Knowledge
