**Karsgard** is a curved [[Planes of Existence|plane]] about 12 million squares miles in size, curving eight inches in a mile. Meaning that in standard [[weather]] conditions, the distance to the horizon is about 3 miles. It's domed off by the [[Sky Above]] beyond which is the vast [[Astral Sea]]. And its overlapped by the [[Echoes of Our World]], as well as directly bordering the [[Worlds of Quintessence]].

Karsgard's [[Calendar]] consists of twelve months, and its days vary in length from 4 hours of sunlight in [[Midwinter]], to 20 hours of sunlight during the last week of [[Midnightsun]]. With a full rotation of the sun taking 24 hours every day.

Broadly speaking, the [[Geography & Politics|geography]] of Karsgard can be divided into [[Seven Realms]] and [[Seven Seas]]. Nearly every corner of which is settled by the countless [[Species & Cultures]] of the [[Forbidden Sphere]].
