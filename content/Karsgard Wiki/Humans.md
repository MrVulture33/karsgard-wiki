>
> On the _Nature of Humans; Their Power and their Ambition_
> by Prof. Englobert Dinklebinkle, Professor of Life Studies in the [[College of Loravalur]]

Out of all the separate [[Species & Cultures|Species]] that inhabits the vast world of [[Karsgard]], the Humans are not just the most prominent, but also the only ones actually native to the [[Common World]]. A majority of the [[Seven Realms]] are thick with Humans, their kingdoms, their trade, their craft, or their kin.

They are known for their trade, their ambition, and ingenuity. They are short-lived compared to [[Dwarves]] and [[Elves]], but perhaps it is because of their short lives that they strive for greatness.

## Cultures
_For where each culture can be found, see the [[Human Culture Map]]_

In Karsgard, there are a number of different Human cultures. While each culture is associated with a particular homeland, because of immigration a person of any culture could find themselves in any other culture's homeland. Furthermore, migration might mean that a person looks like a person of one culture, but behaves like a person from another. That is to say that a woman might look Takayan, behave Fendrian, have a Caelid name, and live in Atrexia.

- [[Takayan]] people of the vast and boiling hot [[Takay]] desert.
- [[Fendrian]] people of the petty feudal kingdoms of [[Fendarana]].
- [[Atrexian]] people of the powerful and progress-oriented [[Atrexia]].
- [[Manchegan]] people of the crimson and dour lands of [[La Manchaland]].
- [[Bastillan]] people of the wild untamed forests of [[Bastilla]].
- [[Caelid]] people from the ancient cradle of democracy in [[Caelida]].
- [[Cathainian]] people from the scenic lands of [[Riverfell]].
- [[Casterian]] people of the cold and distant [[Casterian Isles]].
- [[Druconian]] people of the rebellious feudal kingdom of [[Drucon]].
- [[Easterrian]] people of the far eastern empire of [[Hwei Ming]].
- [[Entorran]] people of the savannahs in [[Northern Areston]].
- [[Khergolian]] people of the great steppe-lands in [[Khergolia]].
- [[Oneirian]] people of the wild [[Darkwood]] forest in [[Northern Areston]].
- [[Ragheani]] people of the prosperous farmlands in [[Raghe]].
- [[Shinkailen]] people of the honor bound shogunate in [[Shinkai]].

## Origins
The origins of humans is a hotly debated subject in the field of [[Academics]], a great many [[Religions]] claim to know the answer, but scholars have yet to find a theory that fits the facts. One thing is clear, humans have been in Karsgard longer than any other humanoid species, they're the only species native to this plane. This means they've been here since before the [[Convergence]]. As a result, they have the following ability:

**Sense Magic.** While within 60 feet of a [[magic item]], a human can taste a metallic tinge in the air, recognizing it immediately as a magical item. Additionally, while in the presence of incredibly powerful [[magic]], or when surrounded by [[The Warp|warp]], a human might also taste a similar tinge in the air.

## Appearance
Humans come in all shapes and sizes, they typically range from 5 feet to little over 6 feet tall, though some are shorter and others taller. They typically weigh between 125 to 250 pounds, though there are exceptions. Human skin shades range from nearly black to very pale, and hair colors from black to blond (curly, kinky, or straight). Males might sport facial hair that is sparse or thick. Humans reach adulthood in their late teens and rarely live even a single century.

## Religion
_For more on this subject, see [[Human Religions]]_

Human [[Religions|Religion]] has its foundations in their various diverse [[cultures]], but due to migration, trade, and conquest, it's not uncommon to stumble upon followers of one religion inside the borders of another's culture. The two largest organized religions are the [[Twelve Sons of Patrem]] and the [[Church of the Mother]].

## Homelands
Humans are found across the entire plane, with each culture having occupied and ruled a large territory of land for the majority of the [[Modern Period]]. They are entirely dominant in three of the [[Seven Realms]]; in [[Atrexia]], the [[Casterian Isles]], and the [[Old World]]
