> Be industrious, let thine eyes be open, lest you become a beggar, for the man that is idle cometh not to honor.
>
> - An ancient Takayan proverb.

The incredibly prosperous Takayan people are one of the oldest [[Humans|human]] [[Species & Cultures|cultures]]. In their day, they had an [[Takay Empire|empire]] that spread to colonies to the north and the south. Today, their land has just been reclaimed from under the tyranny of the [[Black Pharaoh]] and the effort to rebuild the area is being led by the newly crowned Queen [[Samala Nile]].

## Origins
There have been people in the [[Takay]] since before the [[Convergence]]. Their culture however started developing during the [[Rule of the Many Dragon Kings]] under the reign of the [[Dragon King Asmartarta]]. These proto-takayans would continue to develop their customs after Astmartarta's death at the hands of [[Razul]].

During the early [[Modern Period]], however, their culture developed further as the [[Takay Empire]] spread across the [[Takay]] desert and then across [[Karsgard]]. They built great pyramids, temples to their gods, and crowned pharaohs to rule over them.


## Appearance
A typical Takayan might have a skin tone of a reddish-brown colors, dark hair, and brown eyes. While in their homeland, they might wear a small collection of silk clothing, sheer fabrics, with lots of skin showing. They have a particular love of jewelry of colored glass and precious stones, such as headpieces, necklaces, and bracelets. Additionally, the culture as a whole have a love for thick dark eyeliner; on both men and women.


## Religion
Takayans are among the most actively religious humans in the [[Common World]], they make regular tributes and rituals to their gods, most commonly known as [[Djinn]]. It's common for them to pray five times a day, more during times of strike. Their official state religion is the [[Duatt]].


## Homeland
The Takayan homeland is in the [[Takay]];  a vast and boiling hot desert landscape on the continent of [[Valoria]] in the [[Old World]]. The entity of their society is built around the rare rivers and oasis found in the desert landscape.

There's an enclave of Takayan people in [[Redyard]] who live in the old city of [[Alqadiim]]. They are often referred to as Redfolk by the local residents and tend to the treated as second-class citizens alongside the [[Entorran]] people.
