As a part of the [[Atrexian Armed Forces]]; the **Special Tactics Branch**, sometimes called SPEC-TAC, is responsible for covert and paramilitary missions on behalf of the [[Chief of Command]].

Special Tactics Branch typically entrusts their men to embed into officially neutral territory and carry out special missions, they do not wear uniforms or carry standardized equipment.

A common use of SPEC-TAC is to bypass the [[Atrexian Legislation]] that forbids the [[Atrexian Army]] from taking any actions against non-military targets, or operating in non-hostile territories. That doesn't mean that they can do what they want, as being discovered operating in neutral territory can end up starting a war, and is to be avoided at all costs.

This Branch is in command of the following subdivisions, which the [[Missions Administrator]] is free to use with relatively little oversight.

- [[Expeditionary Group]]
- [[Reconnaissance Group]]
- [[Communications Group]]
- [[Irregular Warfare Group]]

## Ranks in the Special Tactics Branch

- **Commander** - In charge of a subdivision, carries out logistics and planning, liaisons between subdivisions and the Missions Administrator.
- **Leftenant** - In charge of a unit, answers to the subdivision's commander.
- **Sergeant**- In charge of a squad, answers to their respective Leftenant.
- **Operator** - A specialized soldier, usually an expert in a complex skill or know-how.
