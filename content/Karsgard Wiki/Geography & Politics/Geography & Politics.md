[[index|Welcome]], dear reader! As you might've guessed, our multiverse is big. Very big. So this section of notes on both **Geography & Politics** might seem ambitious, but here we are.

The [[Forbidden Sphere]] is home to nearly two dozen [[Planes of Existence]], each of whom has a unique ecosystem of wildlife, native species, and geography. While they all swirl somewhere around the [[Astral Sea]], they can be broadly divided into three categories;

- The [[Echoes of Our World]]
- The [[Worlds of Quintessence]]
- The [[Worlds Beyond the Astral]]

Our homeworld sits underneath the [[Sky Above]]. We call it the [[Mundane World of Karsgard]]; which can broadly be divided into its [[Seven Realms]] and [[Seven Seas]].

Though, of course it's nearly impossible to talk about the size and shape of our world without discussing the politics of it, so you'll find the following articles on [[Systems of Government]], [[Feudalism]], [[Currency]], and [[Law]] all a good place to start.
