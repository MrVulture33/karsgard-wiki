In feudal [[Drucon]], you'd find the **Druconians**; a [[Species & Cultures|culture]] of [[Humans]] famous for their elaborate palaces, verdant gardens, and golden symphony halls. All that finery juxtaposed with the squalor of the peasant class living in derelict huts, eeking out a living while working as serfs for their liege lord. A crushing system the peasants have organized numerous revolts to overturn, only to find themselves creating new tyrannies.

## Origins
During the [[Era of the Petty Kingdoms]], during the [[Long Peace]], King Philip II granted his nephew all lands he could settle east of [[Wodenshire]]. Upon the King's death, the Earl of Drucon was coronated as King of Fendrian, starting the [[War of the Succession]]. Wars between [[Fendrian]] and Drucon were a constant from there on out. Repeated again during [[King Crowley's war for independence]] and again during the [[First War for Andruum|First]] and [[Second War for Andruum|Second Wars for Andruum]].

## Appearance
Druconians typically have skin tones ranging from fair to tawny. Their eyes typically green or blue. Hair colors ranging from light to almost black. Their dress sense is usually exemplified by flowing robes, puffy shoulders, intricate patterns, and flamboyant hats.

## Religion
Druconians are fiercely religious. They believe that the [[Twelve Sons of Patrem]] control their life directly and indirectly, and they spend a great deal of time praying to them, enacting rituals, and keeping up with superstitious behavior they fear might've angered their gods.

## Homeland
Drucon is famous for its gorgeous palaces, plazas, and cities of marble, and equally famous for the dirty brown villages, that dot the landscape between the vast swathes of farmland that creates a checkered pattern on the countryside.
