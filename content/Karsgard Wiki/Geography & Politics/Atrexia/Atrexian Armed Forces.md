The **Atrexian Armed Forces** is run by the [[Chief of Command]] and it's split into five separate
branches.

- The [[Atrexian Army]], run by the [[Field Marshal]]
- The [[Atrexian Navy]], run by the [[Grand Admiral]]
- The [[Colonial Guard]], managed by the [[Colonel General]]
- [[National Defense Service]], managed by the [[Undersecretary]] of Defense
- [[Special Tactics Branch]], run by the [[Missions Administrator]]

These branches are supported by the following offices:

- [[Office of Weapons and Munitions]]
- [[Office of Veteran Care]]
