**Starkheim** is the home of the [[Starkhelm Clan]], in the [[Narandia Mountains]]. It's been in decline for decades, and it fell to catastrophe a few months ago.

The heart of this mountain connects to the Mine of Fallen Clans, the Forsaken Forge, Vexerdinanicus' Lair, and the Old Quarry in the hills.
