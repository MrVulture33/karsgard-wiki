The **President** of [[Atrexia]] is the [[Executive Authority]] of the [[Atrexian Republic]]. They are elected
by a majority of the nation during an [[Executive Election]] every four years. Their residence is
the [[Obsidian Palace]] in [[Blackguard]], and from it they oversee the government of the nation.

The President appoints a [[Vice President]], Secretaries to the [[General Advisory Committee]],
Judges to both the [[Courts of Appeal]] and the [[Supreme Court]], and they command the military
through conference with the [[Chief of Command]] of the [[Atrexian Armed Forces]].

The word comes from the meaning to **Preside** over the power of the [[Emperor]], who was
removed from office with the [[Monarchy Abolition Act of 1424]], and the Presidency created
with the [[Reformation Act of 1430]]. Though, the word had been used during the [[Tradian Republic]] to represent those who Presided over the power of the King.

The Presidents of Atrexia are:

- 1443 to 1451: [[Robert Domilius]]
- 1439 to 1443: [[Vivian Klein]]
- 1431 to 1439: [[Trevor Rosenthal]]

## Powers of the Presidency
me President of Atrexia presides over a number of government functions directly, namely:

- The [[Office of the President]], as operated by the [[President's Chief of Staff]].
- The [[Empirical Guard]], as operated by the [[Lord Commander of the Empirical Guard]].
- The [[Undersecretary for Intelligence]] overseeing the [[International Intelligence Committee]].
- The [[Undersecretary]] for the [[Office of Legislative Liaison]].
- The [[Undersecretary]] for the [[Bureau of Criminal Analysis]].
