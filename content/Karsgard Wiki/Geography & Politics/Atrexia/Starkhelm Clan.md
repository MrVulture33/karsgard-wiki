One of the [[Factions in Loggers County|Factions]] in [[Loggers County]] is the **Starkhelm Clan**, an ancient clan of [[Dwarves]] who make their home in the [[Narandia Mountains]], or the Starkhelm Mountains as they call them. These Dwarves operate forges within the mountains and have their sights on mining in the [[Whitecliff Mountains]] as well.

They are lead by King Garrig Starkhelm, who's militant ideas might lead the Starkhelm Clan to war against the [[Nightfont Nomads]] and other tribes in the region.

They see themselves as neither [[Atrexian]] or as [[Blackmoore|Blackmoorish]]. They're proudly independent, and would resist [[Frontier Settlers]]' attempts to categorize them as otherwise.

Their home is [[Starkheim]].
