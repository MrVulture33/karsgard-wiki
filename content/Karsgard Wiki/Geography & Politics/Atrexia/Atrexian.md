**Atrexians** are the most invasive [[Species & Cultures|culture]] of [[Humans|humans]], they enforce their military might, trade will, and culture onto the world of [[Mundane World of Karsgard|Karsgard]]. Their [[Atrexian Navy|navy]] is regularly sighted in foreign harbors, their [[purple coats]] on foreign shores, and their [[Geography & Politics|politics]] infiltrating every comer of the [[map]].

As Atrexians see things; the world used to be a zero-sum game until they discovered the key
to breaking the wheel; progress! Atrexians use the word progress synonymously with words
for good. Progress in banking has given them [[Arthusian Economics]], progress in innovation
has given them the [[Industrial Revolution]], and progress in warfare has given them [[Firearms]].
The world will never be the same.

## Origins
Before the [[Rule of the Many Dragon Kings]], the first ever human city was built on [[Dragonrock]] in the [[Atrexian Sea]]. It was destroyed by [[Razul]] and the few survivors that escaped his wrath
fled to [[Andruum]].

When Razul died, and the [[Rule of the Destroyer]] came to an end, settlers from across Karsgard came to [[Atrexia]] to claim land. [[Fendrian|Fendrians]] settled in the ruins of [[Blackguard]]. [[Casterian|Casterians]]
came from the north, settling in [[Northern Areston]], [[Takayan|Takayans]] came from the south, settling in [[Southem Areston]]. [[Caelid|Caelids]] sailed across through the **Atrexian Sea**, settling in **Dragonrock**,
[[Tradia]], and [[Oakenfall]].

These distinct groups of migrant settlers blended together to form the modern Atrexian , though some of them desperately cling to their historical origins.
