> I appreciate your concerns, but your world is but one of many. And the balance must be maintained everywhere. Not just in your corner of the multiverse.
> 
> - [[Mordenkainen]]

Our multiverse consists of a number of **planes of existence** stacked up against each other, or splayed out like a big pie, or interconnected like branches of a tree. In truth, there are as many interpretation of the shape of our multiverse as there are people studying our multiverse.

Each plane of existence is a reality onto itself, it doesn't obey the rules of other planes, nor does i adhere to their understanding of [[Time]] or [[Space]]. A plane might have a completely unique ecosystem of wildlife, native species, and [[Geography & Politics|geography]]. Not to mention a native type of creature, such as being the home of [[Constructs]], [[Celestials]], [[Fey]], [[Fiends]], or more.

One plane might experience time at the same rate as the [[Mundane World of Karsgard]] while others might experience it faster, slower, not at all, or variations thereupon. The space of a plane can also behave strangely when compared to the Mundane World, while a journey from an initial point A to a terminal point B may seem like a straight line on a map, the shape of some planes may bend, and you'll find yourself ending up at an undetermined point X.

Some elements are common among these planes, such as the [[Wildering Fog]], [[Planar Crossings]], and [[Deities]] of all kinds. And while all planes swirl somewhere around the [[Astral Sea]], and somehow are connected to the [[World Tree of Askur]], they can be broadly divided to three categories:

- [[Mundane World of Karsgard]]
- [[Echoes of Our World]]
- [[Worlds of Quintessence]]
- [[Far Beyond the Astral]]
