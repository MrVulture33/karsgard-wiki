On the westernmost side of the [[Common World]], between [[Casteria]] to the north and [[Quinrell]] to the south, is **Atrexia**. A [[Seven Realms|realm]] named for the tumultuous [[Atrexian Sea]] that surrounds it. Residents of Atrexia are [[Atrexian]].

The realm can be divided into ten [[Geography & Politics|geographical]] sections, the largest landmass is the [[Continent of Areston]], typically divided into four regions [[Northern Areston|north]], [[South]], [[east]], and the [[the center]]. Then the three landmasses [[Dragonrock]], [[Tradia]], and [[Blackmoore]], and thrive island clusters; the [[Freefolk Isles]], the [[Casterian Isles]], and the [[Mid-Sea Islands]].

The realm is home to a number of [[Nation-States]], most of whom localized within their geographic regions, but the [[Atrexian Republic]] is spread across the region.
