> Throughout all my years of study, I have never seen such a thing happen this often. Mechanical constructs gaining sentience? That should be impossible, but here we are, experiencing the beginning of a new **Species** that even the great Professor Dinklebinkle, my grandfather, couldn't have foreseen.
>
> - Zanni Dinklebinkle, Professor of Life Studies in the [[College of Loravalur]]

These ones are the odd ones of the sentient [[Species & Cultures|Species]] that inhabit [[Mundane World of Karsgard|Karsgard]]. They tend to imitate the **culture** or **species** they awaken in, be it [[Dwarves|Dwarven]], [[Elves|Elven]], [[Orcs|Orcish]], or [[Humans|Human]]. Given that they are quite new to the world, they get often described as misfits, freaks, or monsters. However, one thing is clear with these beings: they are full of **wonder** for the world around them.

Their curiosity is unrivaled, leading them to venture into other parts of **Karsgard** where no one else dares to, and allowing them to develop themselves, as they are completely mechanical in their physiology.

## Cultures
Even though awakened constructs adapt to the cultures they awaken in, there has been a narrow type of constructs that awaken:

- [[Mannikins]] that were built for theatrical, sentinel, or technical uses.
- [[Automatons]] that were built for warfare, housing, or infiltration environments.

## Origins
Not much is known on how these constructs awaken into sentience, but it is theorized by some [[College of Loravalur|scholars]] that the effects of the [[The Warp|Warp]] may be involved, giving them some kind of psychological heightening that develops into a mind of its own.
