Unlike from [[Humans]] and [[Dwarves]], **Elves** are not the most populous [[Species & Cultures|Species]] in the vast world of [[Mundane World of Karsgard|Karsgard]], even if they are one of the most dominant. This is due to their long lifespan, allowing them to spend decades and even centuries alive along their families, all while learning and experiencing the world with a resource that many other **Species** lack: [[Time]].

They are vastly known for their love of knowledge. Whether this is true in all of their cultures is a very argued topic, but most seem to agree that there is an innate love of knowledge within all Elves.
