> Where Humans, Dwarves, Elves, Orcs, and Smallkolk have very clear purposes and objectives, the Lost Fey are the other way around. Why do they lower themselves to our level? Why aren't they as mischievous as their Higher relatives? Do they enjoy toying with us in this way? We don't know, and we will probably never know.
>
> *Origin of Fey: Understanding the Ununderstandable*
> by Englebert Dinklebinkle, Professor of Life Studies in the [[College of Loravalur]]

The [[Fey]] are one of the most misunderstood beings on these [[Planes of Existence|planes]], and one proof of this are those Lesser ones that inhabit our [[Mundane World of Karsgard|plane]]. Wild, unpredictable, and at the same time as close to a humanoid form of thinking as they can get, these **Fey** that live alongside us make our civilization more interesting to explore.

## Cultures
In **Karsgard**, there are a number of Lost Fey cultures. Each culture has a different form, behavior and subcultures. Each subculture having adapted pretty well to its surroundings, making them perfect for those specific scenarios.

- [[Goblinoids]]
- [[Theriomorphs]]
- [[Shapeshifters]]
