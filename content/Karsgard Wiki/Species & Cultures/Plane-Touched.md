> Is difficult to find creatures as interesting as those that are Plane-Touched. Having your whole existence be defined by your inheritance tends to be common for them, and rejecting or accepting that inheritance is always fascinating, when seen from afar of course.
>
> *Connection between Planes: Outer beings and their Descendants*
> Englebert Dinklebinkle, Professor of Life Studies in the [[College of Loravalur]]

This must be one of the more interesting [[Species & Cultures|Species]] to date, given that they are born when someone or something from a plane different to [[Mundane World of Karsgard|Karsgard]] interacts in some way with a inhabitant of the latter, giving birth to several types of individuals, characterized by their innate abilities that connects them to said plane.

## Cultures
**Plane-Touched** don't have a *culture* per se, as they adopt the one they tend to be born in. They do have some difference between each other, as it is obvious that a **Tiefling** would be different from a **Dji**.

- [[Tieflings]]
- [[Dji]]
- [[Genasi]]
- [[Cactar]]
- [[Dragonborn]]

## Origins
Before the [[Convergence]], a lot of beings from other [[Planes of Existence|Planes]] already interacted with [[Mundane World of Karsgard|Karsgard]]. When *it* happened, it opened the door for these beings to interact more directly, allowing them to leave their own kind of descendants in the [[Common World]].
