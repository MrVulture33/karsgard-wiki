The **Atrexian Republic** is a [[Nation-States|nation-state]] in the [[Seven Realms|realm]] of [[Atrexia]] [[Geography & Politics|geographically]] located in the [[Atrexian Sea]]. It's the successor nation of the [[Oldfield Empire]], reorganized by the [[Reformation Act of 1430]].

## Three Branches of Government
The **Atrexian Republic** has three branches of government derived from its [[constitution]]: the *Legislative Branch*, the *Judicial Branch*, and the *Executive Branch*. Together, they [[check and balance]] each other, while no single entity has supreme authority over the nation state.

The **Legislative Branch** is the [[Atrexian Parliament]], a body of representatives called [[Chancellors]] who are elected during a [[General Election]] to represent the common people and draft legislation in their name, these are called [[Acts of Parliament]], and they become law when signed into law by the President. Parliament holds internal elections to choose a [[First Chancellor]] to represent them at meetings of the [[General Advisory Committee]].

The **Judicial Branch** is the [[Imperial Judiciary]], which is comprised of [[District Courthouses]], the [[Courts of Appeal]], and finally the [[Supreme Court]], the elected leader of which becomes the [[Lord Executive Magistrate]] and sits on meetings of the [[General Advisory Committee]].

The **Executive Branch** is lead by the [[President of Atrexia]], an elected representative elected via [[Executive Election]]. Once elected the President then appoints a [[Vice President of Atrexia]] and assembles the General Advisory Committee to run the executive branch of the government. Immediate resolutions to crisis are the direct purview of the President, as such, draft [[Executive Resolutions]], or [[Veto Legislation]], both of which carry the weight of constitutional by the **Imperial Judiciary**, or overruled by a [[ruling]] made by the [[Atrexian Parliament]].
